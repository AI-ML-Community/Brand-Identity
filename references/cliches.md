# Clichés to ban

Before drawing, write down the banned list for the product's category, plus any trope a quick look at three competitors reveals. A concept that uses one of these must turn it into something only this product could own. Otherwise, kill it.

## Everywhere
- Letter in a circle or rounded square, set in a stock font
- Abstract swoosh, orbit ring or infinity loop
- Globe, network of dots and lines, hexagon cluster
- Gradient orb, glassmorphism, 3D blob, holographic sheen
- Generic "spark", star or burst
- Monogram made by overlapping two letters with transparency
- Puzzle piece, lightning bolt, rocket, target or bullseye
- A mascot as the only idea

## Learning, reading, education
- Open book, stack of books, graduation cap, owl
- Lightbulb, brain, pencil, apple, chalkboard
- Page with a folded corner, text lines, highlighter stroke

## AI, developer tools
- Sparkles, magic wand, four-point star
- Neural-network nodes, circuit traces, chip outline
- Chat bubble with dots, cursor and brackets `</>`, terminal prompt `>_`
- Robot head, eye, gradient purple-to-blue anything

## Finance, fintech, payments
- Upward arrow or chart line, coin, stack of coins, piggy bank
- Shield, lock, check mark, wallet, card outline

## Health, fitness, wellness
- Heart, pulse line, cross, leaf, lotus, person mid-stride, water drop

## Social, messaging, community
- Speech bubbles, people silhouettes, hands, handshake, hearts, map pin

## Commerce, delivery, travel
- Shopping bag, cart, box, truck, map pin, paper plane, suitcase, airplane

## Productivity, calendars, tasks
- Check box, check mark, calendar grid, clock face, stacked cards, kanban columns

## Media, music, video
- Play triangle, waveform, headphones, film strip, camera aperture, music note

## How to escape a cliché instead of just avoiding it
- Find the **ritual** only this product has (LearnitPal: one pick a day, and it keeps your place) and draw the object from that ritual.
- **Fuse it with a letter** from the name so the shape becomes ownable.
- Put the **accent on the one element that carries the promise**, never on decoration.
- **Run the swap test again.** If a competitor could still use it, keep going.
